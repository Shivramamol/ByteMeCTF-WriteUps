## Challenge Description

> **Mission: The Traveller's Trail**  
> What is the old name of the fort the traveller hiked?

## Summary

The traveller hiked a pyramid-shaped hill fort of approximately 3,500 feet elevation near Tikona Peth, which corresponds to **Tikona Fort**. Historical and geographical records document its traditional and ancient name as **Vitandgad**.

## Step 1 — Identify the Fort from Geographic Characteristics

The traveller's note describes the fort:

> *"The fort was around 3,500 ft high. It looked almost like a pyramid from a distance.*  
> *It took us about half an hour to climb. We basically raced to the top."*

Characteristics:
- Height: ~3,500 ft (Tikona has an elevation of ~3,500 ft / 1,066 m).
- Distinct shape: Prominent triangular/pyramidal profile visible across Maval / Pawna dam region.
- Location: Accessible from Tikona Peth.

This confirms the fort is **Tikona Fort**.

## Step 2 — Find the Ancient / Alternate Name

Researching the history of Tikona Fort reveals:
- "Tikona" literally refers to its triangular/pyramid shape (*triangular* in Marathi).
- The fort's historical Maratha and medieval name is **Vitandgad**.
