>
> Reconstruct the journey step by step—from the railway station to the trek, the forts, the lake, and finally the traveller's digital footprint.

> Follow the trail. Find the flags. Complete the journey.

## Trail Summary

1. **Station 1 (Akurdi):** Identifying the local railway station near Nigdi, Pradhikaran.
2. **Station 2 (Kamshet):** Counting 6 intermediate stops along the Pune–Lonavala local line.
3. **Village (Tikona Peth):** Tracing the ~20–22 km bus ride to the fort's base village.
4. **Fort Name (Vitandgad):** Identifying the pyramid-shaped peak of Tikona Fort and its historic name.
5. **Horizon Forts (Lohagad & Visapur):** Comparing the summit vista with regional terrain and sightlines.
6. **Lake & Fort (Tung Fort & Pawna Lake):** Geolocating the lake and conical fort on the return leg.
7. **Digital Footprint (Google Maps Review):** Discovering the planted review by `glitchseeker` and decoding the Base64 string.
8. **Identity (Sarthak Warale — Co-Lead):** Correlating the user profile with CTFtime and the official OWASP PCCOE team page.
